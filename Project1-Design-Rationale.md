# Project 1: Business Website UX Redesign — Design Rationale

**Business:** Meridian Advisory (fictional business consulting firm)
**Objective:** Redesign a business website to improve usability, clarity, and conversion rates.

## 1. Visual Hierarchy (Homepage)
The homepage follows a strict top-to-bottom reading order: eyebrow label → bold headline → one-line supporting text → single primary CTA → supporting hero visual. Font size, weight, and color contrast (navy headline vs. muted grey body text) guide the eye without needing borders or boxes.

## 2. Navigation Structure
Reduced navigation to 4 core items (Services, Case Studies, About, Contact) plus one CTA button. Fewer choices reduce decision fatigue and keep the header scannable in under 2 seconds — important since most visitors decide whether to stay within the first few seconds.

## 3. Responsive Wireframes (Desktop + Mobile)
- **Desktop:** 3-column service cards, horizontal nav, side-by-side CTA banner.
- **Mobile:** Cards stack to 1 column, nav collapses to a hamburger menu, CTA banner stacks and centers text. Touch targets (buttons) are kept at least 44px tall for easy tapping.

## 4. CTA Placement
Two CTAs are placed deliberately:
1. **Hero CTA** — for visitors who already know they want help.
2. **Post-services CTA banner** — for visitors who needed to see what Meridian offers before deciding. This catches users who scroll past the first CTA without converting.

Both CTAs use the *same* accent color (amber) and same wording ("Book a Free Consultation") so the action feels consistent and recognizable, not like two different asks.

## 5. Typography & Spacing Consistency
- Display font (serif) used only for headlines — keeps them feeling authoritative and distinct from body copy.
- Body font (sans-serif) used for all paragraph and UI text for readability at small sizes.
- Consistent 24–40px section padding rhythm keeps the page feeling calm rather than cramped.

## 6. Professional Visual Style
A single accent color (amber) is reserved only for actionable elements (buttons, key labels) — everything else stays in navy/ink/grey. This keeps the design feeling deliberate and premium rather than colorful/noisy, which suits a B2B advisory audience.

## Outcome
The redesign creates a clear path: **headline → understand value → see proof → see services → convert**, with no competing calls to action and a layout that holds up cleanly on both desktop and mobile.
